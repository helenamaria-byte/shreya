The Nine Checks — Does The Dispatch Deserve to Be Built?

🛑 Kill Switches

Any No means: don't build it.

  • 01 Problem real? YES — Shreya spends four hours a week reading 20+ sources and 60+ articles to make one issue.
  • 02 Workflow repeated? YES — every week, Monday to Thursday. Same read, shortlist, write, assemble.
  • 03 Input available? YES — live RSS, Google News and GNews feeds, her last four issues and her style guide.

⚖️ Sizing

Any No means: build something smaller.

  • 04 Output valuable? YES — a ready draft: 8–10 stories, 3+ topics, 2–3 lines each in her voice, in Telegram by Wednesday.
  • 05 Impact measurable? YES — review time vs four hours, stories kept vs swapped, delivered, bounced and unsubscribed.
  • 08 ROI worth it? YES — free tiers (Gemini Flash, Neon, Vercel, Resend). Built once, runs every week for 2,200 readers.

🔒 Boundary

Any No means: put a human exactly here.

  • 06 Failure risk OK? YES — worst case is a weak draft she swaps or rejects. Nothing is emailed without her confirm.
  • 07 Judgment protected? YES — Gemini shortlists and drafts. Shreya keeps, edits, swaps and confirms every story.
  • 09 Owner clear? YES — the final call on every issue is Shreya's, in her Telegram. The system owns collection and delivery.
“Just let Gemini send it every Thursday.” → Killed by 07. The AI drafts. Shreya decides.
“Let anyone approve from the dashboard.” → Killed by 09. Approval lives only in Shreya's Telegram.
